Dashcut support

Dashcam clips from your comma drives.

What you need

A comma device paired to a comma account, and an iPhone or iPad on iOS 18 or newer. You can look around a demo drive without any of that: tap Look around a demo drive on the first screen.

Signing in

Use the same account you use at connect.comma.ai, through GitHub, Google or Apple. Dashcut never sees your password.

Making a clip

Open a drive, drag the two handles on the timeline to the stretch you want, and press Export. You can take the three cameras together, stacked one above the other, or any one of them on its own. Clips land in your photo library.

Two ways to get the footage

My comma asks the device to encode the clip itself and send it over. It is quick and uses hardly any data, but the comma has to be awake and online, on software 0.11.2 or newer.

comma's servers builds the clip from what comma has already uploaded. It works with the car parked, but it is a large download, roughly 37 MB a minute for each camera.

There is no sound on my clip

openpilot only records the microphone into its small preview file, and only when the microphone setting is on. Turn on Record Microphone in your comma's settings and later drives will have audio. Drives recorded before that will not.

A drive says nothing has uploaded

comma uploads a small preview of every drive once the device is online. Full quality footage stays on the device until something asks for it. Use My comma as the source, or request an upload in comma connect and try again later.

Something else

Write to open an issue on GitHub, and say what you were doing and which comma you have.
