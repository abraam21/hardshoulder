Dashcut privacy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Dashcut is a viewer for footage recorded by your own comma device. The short version: Dashcut collects nothing about you, and sends nothing anywhere except to comma, on your behalf.

What Dashcut stores

When you sign in, comma gives the app a sign-in token. That token is kept in the iOS keychain on your phone and nowhere else. Signing out deletes it. Dashcut does not create an account of its own and has no server.

What Dashcut sends, and where

Dashcut talks only to comma's own services at api.comma.ai and athena.comma.ai, using your token, to list your drives, play them back, and ask your device for a clip. What you do in the app is covered by comma's privacy policy. Nothing is sent to the developer of Dashcut.

Your footage

Video and position data are downloaded to your phone to show you a drive and to build a clip. Clips you export are written to your photo library because you asked for them. None of it leaves your phone unless you share it yourself.

Analytics and tracking

There are none. No analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no third-party software development kits, no identifiers for tracking.

Children

Dashcut is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Deleting your data

Signing out removes the token from your phone. Deleting the app removes everything Dashcut has stored. Your drives belong to your comma account, so delete those at connect.comma.ai.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
